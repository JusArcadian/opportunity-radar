Install Radar on your phone

iPhone: open this page in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Android: tap the menu (⋮), then Install app.

Back up your data

Radar keeps your saved jobs on this phone only. Download a backup file now and then, and load it on a new phone. A full backup replaces what is here; an import file (like one made from your Google Sheet) only adds to it.

Where jobs come from

Remote jobs: Himalayas, Remotive and Jobicy. Entry-level jobs near you: The Muse. Local jobs: Jobs by Adzuna (when turned on). Pay ranges compared with U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics data. Radar never applies for you.

About Radar and your privacy

Radar is free. It finds job postings, helps you track what you applied to, and builds resumes from facts you give it.

  • No account. Everything you type stays on this phone.
  • Your resume is read on this phone and is never uploaded.
  • Radar never applies for you and never messages anyone.
  • Jobs come from public job sites. Radar isn't part of any of them, and can't promise a posting is real, so check the red flags.
  • Pay and take-home numbers are estimates. Your real paycheck can be different.
